and its publisher.

## Forks and modified distributions

Substantially modified forks should use their own product name and visual
identity. Avoid presenting a fork as an official Shadowfetch release or
implying that Shadowfetch sponsors, approves, or maintains it. Do not use
Shadowfetch or ShadowCode logos as the primary identity of a different product.
Clearly identify the publisher of your distribution and describe your changes.
Keep the license, NOTICE, and other attribution required by the Apache license.

Accurate attribution and truthful descriptions are permitted, including
“Based on ShadowCode by Shadowfetch.” Attribution does not imply endorsement.

## Community references and integrations

Ordinary community references, reviews, tutorials, screenshots, and compatible
integrations may use the names to identify the project accurately. Describe
compatibility truthfully and make clear who provides an integration. These uses
must not imply official sponsorship or endorsement where none exists.
